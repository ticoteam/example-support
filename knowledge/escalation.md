# Escalation

What must reach a person immediately, and who. Replace the names with your own roster.

| What it is | Who | How fast |
|---|---|---|
| Money owed, refund, chargeback, credit request | support owner (`owner@acme.example`) | before the pass ends |
| Legal wording, a threat to sue, a regulator, a data-protection request | the founder | before the pass ends |
| Security: a leaked credential, an account takeover, "someone else can see my data" | engineering on-call | immediately |
| Safety of a person | the founder | immediately |
| An angry long-standing customer | support owner | same day |
| A bug with a reproducible path | the engineering owner, as a task with the steps | same day |
