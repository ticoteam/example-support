# Standing answers

One entry per question that keeps coming back: the question as customers ask it, the answer, the date
it was last confirmed, and who confirmed it. A draft uses an answer as written and adjusts it to what
the customer actually asked. An answer nobody has confirmed in six months is a question, not an answer.

## How do I reset my password?
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page; the link arrives within a few minutes and expires after an
hour. If it does not arrive, check spam, then tell us which address you signed up with.
