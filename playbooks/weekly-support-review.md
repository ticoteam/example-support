# Weekly support review

Routine, once a week, once a person has read a few weeks of passes and found the review useful.
Budget 30 minutes. The outcome is a short list a person can act on, and answers that are current.

1. List the tickets handled this week from the task notes (ids and buckets only).
2. Count by bucket. Which known issues grew? Which "New" tickets look alike enough to become a
   standing answer? Draft those answers into `knowledge/answers.md` marked `unconfirmed` until a
   person confirms them.
3. Find answers whose last-confirmed date is older than six months and list them for review.
4. Find tickets that waited longest for a person and say why (missing owner, unclear escalation).
5. Write `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-support-review.md`: counts, top three recurring problems with ticket ids,
   answers added, answers needing confirmation. Never include customer personal details.
6. Finish with one task to the support owner: the top recurring problem and what fixing it would
   remove from the queue.
